# Chinese editing examples

## A notice people can act on

**Source notes.** The library's second-floor reading room will close for lighting work on December 5 from 14:00 to 16:00. The first-floor return desk stays open.

**Before.** 为进一步优化阅读环境，本馆将组织开展照明设施更换工作，届时部分区域暂停对外开放，敬请广大读者合理安排到馆时间。

**After.** 12 月 5 日 14:00–16:00，图书馆二楼阅览室换灯，暂停开放。只来还书的话，一楼还书台照常开放。

The revision uses the source's exact place and time. Readers can see whether their visit is affected.

## A WeChat opening built around a real result

**Source.** The authors of *Depth Pro 2* report that the S model processes 1080p streaming video at 53 frames per second on one NVIDIA B200. The H+ model runs at about 8 frames per second in this setting. [Paper, Figure 12](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26603v1)

**Loose draft.** 苹果的新模型能够以每秒 53 帧实时处理高清视频，深度估计性能大幅提升。

**After.** 苹果的 Depth Pro 2 能从普通视频估计物体离镜头多远。论文里的速度要看具体型号。同样在一张 NVIDIA B200 上逐帧处理 1080p 视频，小号 S 版每秒处理 53 帧，最大号 H+ 版每秒约 8 帧。如果你想用它做实时预览，先看选中的型号能否跟上输入视频的帧率。

The rewritten opening puts test conditions next to the numbers. The last sentence gives the reader a way to apply them.

## A line edit with a missing date

**Source notes.** The team moved the registration deadline. The new date is pending. Confirmed registrations remain valid.

**Requested edit.** 把这段报名提醒写短一点。已经报名的人最关心要不要重报。

**After.** 已确认报名的读者可以照原计划参加。报名截止时间已调整。

**To verify.** 补充新的报名截止日期和具体时间。

The gap stays visible to the person preparing the announcement. Readers get the confirmed action in the opening.
