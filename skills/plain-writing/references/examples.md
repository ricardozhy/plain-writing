# Worked editing examples

The examples below use fictional writing samples and research figures.

## A day-to-day email in Chinese

**Notes.** Send the revised budget sheet by Friday at 15:00. Ask the recipient to check the equipment totals in column C against the receipts.

**Before.** 为保证预算审核工作的顺利开展，请在规定时间内对相关材料进行核查并及时反馈。

**After.** 周五 15:00 前，麻烦把改好的预算表发给我。请对照收据，核一下 C 列的设备费用合计。

The recipient can find the deadline, deliverable, and place to check without asking for the surrounding conversation.

## An English message with an unknown detail

**Notes.** The team will meet remotely on Tuesday. The meeting link is pending. Attendees should send questions by Monday at 17:00.

**Draft.** We’ll meet remotely on Tuesday. Please send your questions by 5 p.m. on Monday.

**To verify.** Add the meeting link before sending.

The draft gives attendees the confirmed action. The missing link stays with the person preparing the message.

## A measured result in an English paper

**Notes.** A log-retrieval method evaluated 300 held-out failed builds from one repository. The relevant log appeared among the first 10 results for 68% of builds with the new method and 61% with the baseline. The study did not measure debugging time.

**Before.** The proposed framework significantly improves debugging performance across repositories and provides a more efficient experience.

**After.** On 300 held-out failed builds from one repository, our method retrieved the relevant log in the top ten for 68% of builds, compared with 61% for the baseline.

The revision states the measured outcome and includes the comparison beside it. The reported outcome is retrieval performance.

## A reply to a reviewer

**Notes.** The reviewer asked why the four test folds were built by time. The authors added a paragraph to Section 3.2. Each fold trains on earlier events and tests on the next month.

**Before.** We thank the reviewer for raising this insightful point, which we have comprehensively addressed in the revised version.

**After.** We split events chronologically to evaluate each model on data collected after its training period. Section 3.2 now specifies that each fold trains on earlier events and uses the following month for testing.

The reply gives the reason and tells the reviewer where to find the added detail.
